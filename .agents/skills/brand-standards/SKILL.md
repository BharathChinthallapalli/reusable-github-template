## Checklist

1. Obtain the authorized organization's source document, owner, URL or repository path, version and review date; do not invent brand policy.
2. Separate mandatory brand constraints from optional style preferences and legal/AI transparency requirements.
3. Map approved typography, colors, terminology, tone and accessibility-compatible usage to acceptance criteria.
4. Flag contradictions to the named brand/UX owner; do not let brand conventions suppress required disclosures.
5. Mark a missing organization standard as unresolved for brand-dependent decisions, not as an invented default.
