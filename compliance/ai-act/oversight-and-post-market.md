# Oversight and post-market plan

Define qualified oversight roles, authority, training, known automation-bias risks,
interpretation support, escalation, override/stop procedure, feedback and review
cadence. Record monitored indicators, dataset/model shifts, incident reporting
responsibility, corrective measures and retirement triggers. Distinguish system
logging capability, retained event data and record-specific retention.

A stop button without tested authorization and containment is not proof of
effective oversight. See runbooks and [matrix](../MATRIX.md).
