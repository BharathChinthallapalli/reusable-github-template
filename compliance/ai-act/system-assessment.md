# Per-system AI Act assessment

System ID/version; intended purposes across technical, contractual and promotional
materials; operator/provider/deployer roles; prohibited-use screening; AI-system
definition; classification route under6(1)/AnnexI or6(2)/AnnexIII; profiling and
material influence; applicable dates and legacy status; policy owner and evidence.

Evaluate Art27 FRIA scope separately from GDPR DPIA. Do not conclude low risk
merely because a human reviews outputs. The AnnexIII filter is not a general
AnnexI exemption. Missing AnnexI draft guidance is not a claim that binding
AnnexI legislation is absent. See [matrix](../MATRIX.md).
