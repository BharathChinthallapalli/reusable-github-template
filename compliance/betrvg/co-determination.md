# Employee-monitoring co-determination note

Before enabling telemetry capable of monitoring worker behavior or performance,
identify employer/establishment, affected employees, purpose, fields, access,
retention, evaluation capability and existing agreements. Engage the authorized
works-council/legal process under applicable §87 conditions. Record agreement,
limitations and review dates; do not silently repurpose SOC logs for productivity
scoring. There is no invented universal retention period in this template.
