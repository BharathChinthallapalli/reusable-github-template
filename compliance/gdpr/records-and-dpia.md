# Processing record and DPIA intake

Create separate records for Guardian/SOC telemetry and each personal-data system:
controller/processor, purpose/legal basis, data-subject and data categories,
recipients/processors, transfers and safeguards, retention/deletion criteria,
access controls, security measures and accountable owner.

Assess likely high risk before processing: systematic profiling/significant
decisions, special categories, monitoring, scale, vulnerable groups, combining
datasets and relevant authority criteria. Record rationale and DPO advice where
applicable. Do not collect personal data simply to fill a template. A company
under250 staff is not automatically exempt from Art30.

AI Act information duties and GDPR notices do not replace applicable works-council
co-determination. Legal basis and retention require case-specific review.
