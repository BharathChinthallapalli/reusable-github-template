# OWASP control review

Use the 2025 LLM Top 10 mapping in [MATRIX](../MATRIX.md) and current agentic guidance.
For each risk record a concrete abuse path, trust boundary, enforcing control,
positive/negative test, residual risk and owner. Revisit after model, retrieval,
MCP, memory or tool-permission changes. Policy text alone is not a tested control.
