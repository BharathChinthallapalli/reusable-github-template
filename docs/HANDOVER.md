# Project handover

Before release, record project ID/archetypes, exact release revision, owners and
on-call contacts, architecture/ADD/spec links, environment/identity inventory,
data/model/prompt versions, operational access, cost envelope and known limits.

Required evidence: human authorization, CI and independent review, applicable
compliance assessment, eval calibration/results, rollout and rollback rehearsal,
kill-switch authority, incident intake, telemetry retention and training.

Record each open finding with owner and due date. A handover document is not a
release authorization, compliance certification or permission to read secrets.
See [activation findings](sdlc/ACTIVATION.md).
