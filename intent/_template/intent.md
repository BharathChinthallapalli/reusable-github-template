# Change intent

## Problem
Describe the evidenced problem, not a preferred implementation.

## Proposed outcome
Describe the measurable user outcome and explicit non-goals.

## Affected users and systems
List system IDs, operator roles, intended purposes, data subjects and archetypes.

## Constraints
Budget, delivery date, staffing, region, existing interfaces and tool boundaries.

## Open questions
Resolve every execution-critical question before admission; record decisions.

## Compliance
Assess AI-system definition, prohibited uses, risk class, provider/deployer role,
GDPR legal basis and DPIA trigger, NIS2 entity scope, and employee monitoring /
BetrVG. A non-applicability entry requires a reason and owner. Metadata above
intentionally blocks rendering until assessed.

## Feasibility
For C/D/E: technical, operational and economic viability; otherwise reasoned N/A.

## Measurable KPIs
Baseline, target, measurement source, cost ceiling and evaluation horizon.

## Ethical impact
Affected groups, disparate impacts, abuse scenarios and mitigation owner.

## Regulatory review
Identify applicable current texts, version/date and required human decisions.
