# Data card — one per versioned dataset

Identity/version/hash; accountable owner; provenance/collection; license and
consent scope; data subjects/classification; access and regions; intended/excluded
use; retention/deletion; lineage/transforms; quality and representativeness;
known bias; train/validation/test separation; contamination checks; update cadence
and downstream consumers. Record missing evidence and limitations.

Do not commit sensitive datasets or secrets. Store approved references and hashes
only. [Google Data Cards](https://sites.research.google/datacardsplaybook/) informs
the worksheet; only its introduction was fully read in this source review.
