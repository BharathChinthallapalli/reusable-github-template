# Incident record / runbook

Incident ID/class, system/version, observed time, evidence and affected users;
containment authority and safe actions; notifications and legal clocks; diagnosis;
root cause versus hypothesis; recovery verification; follow-up intent ID;
required new regression eval; responsible owner and review date.

Do not place credentials, mailbox content or unnecessary personal data in the
record. Guardian denials, pivot attempts and hook timeout fail-open events escalate
immediately; no alternate tool/runtime is an authorized workaround.
